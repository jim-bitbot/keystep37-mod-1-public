## 1. Where the realtime compares are

0x0800fa64 is called from the main loop at 0x080157ae and 0x08015856.
Its 0xF8 use is a range check, blo back to 0x0800faac:

  0x0800fcb6  f82e        cmp      r6, #0xf8
  0x0800fcb8  fff4f8ae    blo.w    #0x0800faac

The clock / start / stop compares are in 0x0800ffe0. r4 is the incoming r0. The byte is [r4].

  0x0800ffe0  2de9f041    push.w   {r4, r5, r6, r7, r8, lr}
  0x0800ffe6  0446        mov      r4, r0
  0x080100c4  2378        ldrb     r3, [r4]
  0x080100c6  fa2b        cmp      r3, #0xfa
  0x080100c8  25d0        beq      #0x08010116
  0x080100ca  fc2b        cmp      r3, #0xfc
  0x080100cc  50d0        beq      #0x08010170
  0x080100ce  fb2b        cmp      r3, #0xfb
  0x080100d0  6cd0        beq      #0x080101ac
  0x080100d2  f82b        cmp      r3, #0xf8
  0x080100d4  a9d1        bne      #0x0801002a

  0xFA  0x08010116  movs r1, #1; bl 0x08012334
  0xF8  0x080100d6  ldr r0, [pc] ; 0x20005534
        0x080100d8  bl 0x0800b0c6

0xFC reads [sp,#0x2c]+0x10 and compares with #2. 0xFB is a fourth beq, target 0x080101ac.

Same four compares again at 0x080104fc, inside 0x08010254. Each beq sets r7=1 and joins at 0x0801038e. That function's only static callers of 0x0800ffe0 are 0x080102fa and 0x080105ae, both inside 0x08010254.

0x08010254 has no static bl. One Thumb pointer: 0x0801e9a8 → 0x08010255.
Entry r2 selects the path:

  0x0801025a  0446        mov      r4, r0
  0x0801025c  0d46        mov      r5, r1
  0x0801025e  1746        mov      r7, r2
  0x08010260  62b3        cbz      r2, #0x080102bc
  0x08010262  012a        cmp      r2, #1
  0x08010264  4cd1        bne      #0x08010300

No USART1 or USB peripheral literal in these windows. 0x20005534 is also the object TIM2 loads (scans/I-time.txt).

## 2. Tap

Compact index 4 is id 0x67 (scans/F-buttons.txt). In all three panel_button_dispatch tables, r1=4 targets the common pop at 0x08017b0e:

  unshifted  half 0x0444
  shifted    half 0x0151
  r2==0      half 0x001d

The only #0x67 immediates in the app are id_to_index 0x08006034 and index_to_id 0x0800608a.
